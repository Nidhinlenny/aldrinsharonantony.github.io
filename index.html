04 / About

Visual storytelling through light, movement & detail.

Aldrin Sharon Antony is a cinematographer, photographer and video editor experienced in creating commercial advertisements, promotional films, product photography and social-media visual content.

His work combines cinematography, photography and post-production to create visuals aligned with the identity and communication goals of each brand.

He works with clients, directors and creative teams across camera operation, lighting, composition, talent direction and post-production.
